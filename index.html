Designed for freedom

Forge your own legacy

No fixed classes

Train every discipline, develop masteries and choose your own equipment.

Tactical combat

Real-time encounters, typed commands, weapon techniques and original magical traditions.

Meaningful craftsmanship

Discover recipes, refine materials and build your reputation as an artisan.

A living economy

Regional markets, trade contracts, businesses and player-driven commerce.

Oaths & allegiances

Build relationships, join organizations and earn your place in society.

Your living chronicle

Discoveries, victories and accomplishments become part of your character's history.
