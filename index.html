The vision

Not a class. Not a destiny. A legacy.

You can become anything. Becoming exceptional at everything is a lifetime's work.

Emberwolf is an independent, command-driven MMORPG designed around freedom, consequence and a shared persistent world. Instead of 3D environments, cinematic typography, animated effects, atmospheric sound and evocative writing bring the world to life. Every character begins with humble equipment and the freedom to pursue their own ambitions.

Character progression

The Forging

Develop your character through practice, knowledge, mastery and reputation rather than selecting a permanent class. Train martial, magical, gathering, crafting and social disciplines on the same character.

Attributes

Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Endurance, Intelligence, Wisdom, Perception, Charisma and Luck.

Disciplines & mastery

Train skills through use, discover advanced techniques and pursue mastery trials. The proposed discipline cap is 100.

Professional focus

Specialize in selected professions for modest efficiency bonuses without permanently locking other professions.

Identity & ancestry

Create up to five characters per account. Planned ancestries include Vaelborn, Ashkari, Sylvaren, Kharuun, Veyrathi and Dravok.

The artisan's mark

Greatness is forged

Crafting is designed as a complete progression path. Material purity, tools, workshops, recipes and mastery influence quality. Exceptional equipment can carry its creator's identity and history.

Gather

Mining, Woodcutting, Herbalism, Fishing, Hunting, Skinning and Farming.

Refine

Smelting, Leatherworking and Weaving turn raw resources into useful materials.

Create

Weaponsmithing, Armorsmithing, Tailoring, Carpentry, Fletching and Jewelcrafting.

Specialize

Alchemy, Enchanting, Inscription and Cooking support adventurers and the economy.

Equipment with a story

Rarity and craftsmanship quality are independent. Items can have durability, material composition, enchantments, requirements and ownership history.

A world shaped by its people

Wealth, power and consequence

Regional commerce

Trade through regional markets, secure contracts and player businesses. Copper Embers, silver Lunars and gold Sovereigns form the planned currency system.

Oaths & allegiances

Join noble houses, military orders, trading houses, artisan guilds or adventuring companies. Earn citizenship, rank and reputation.

Law & danger

Protected sanctuaries and dangerous frontiers offer different risks. Consensual duels, criminal reputation and risk-based PvP are planned.

Chronicles & world omens

Explore optional storylines, contracts, bounties and shared regional events. The first planned chronicle is Whispers Beyond the Veil.

Frequently asked questions

Before you enter Vaelrath

Is Emberwolf playable yet?

No. The standalone MMORPG is in preproduction; there is no public release date.

Is this a Discord game?

No. This project is a standalone Windows game built with Unreal Engine 5. Discord is the community hub.

Will I need to choose a class?

No. The design allows every character to train every discipline and profession.

Will my character persist?

Persistent characters, inventory, progression and world state are core design requirements. Supabase PostgreSQL is the planned persistence layer.

Will I have to participate in PvP?

The design includes protected settlements and peaceful progression paths alongside riskier PvP regions.

Will there be graphics?

The game uses cinematic text, interface animations, particles and audio rather than traditional 3D character models and environments.
